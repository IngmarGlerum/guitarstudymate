Bass and piano samples: FluidR3_GM General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen,
as rendered to MP3 by Benjamin Gleitzman in "midi-js-soundfonts"

Changes: only every third semitone of acoustic_bass (MIDI 28-58),
acoustic_grand_piano and electric_piano_1 (MIDI 43-82) is kept, one MP3 file
per note named by its MIDI number. Other pitches are played by resampling.
